Phase 1a — Basis-Free Spectral Metrics (H3)

▶

Goal

Implement and validate basis-free spectral evaluation metrics that reveal fidelity gaps invisible to standard metrics.

Metrics Implemented

  • Spectral density JSD — KDE on normalized eigenvalues, Jensen-Shannon divergence
  • Quantile-band energy (QBE) — per-band energy comparison across equal-mass quantile bands
  • Heat kernel signature (HKS) distance — Tr(exp(-tL)) curves over logarithmic time grid

Results

G1 gate: GO — QBE distance consistently reveals spectral fidelity gaps invisible to standard MMD, especially for configuration-model baselines (matched degree sequence but wrong spectral structure).

Key finding: QBE is the most discriminative metric: it detects spectral degradation that degree/clustering/triangle MMD completely misses, particularly for baselines with correct degree distributions but incorrect community structure.

H2: Temporal Ramp

Spearman ρ = −0.21, p = 0.74, 95% CI = [−1.0, 1.0]. No correlation between optimal t_knee and spectral gap ratio. QBE curves flat across all t_knee values.

G2 gate: NO-GO — 0/3 families pass Bonferroni-corrected significance test across all three experiments.

Root cause: The 3-layer GCN score network on 200-node graphs cannot exploit spectral noise structure. The bottleneck is model expressiveness, not noise fidelity. At this scale, uniform noise is sufficient because the model cannot resolve spectral structure regardless.

Remaining Options

  • Scale up: Test on larger graphs (>1000 nodes) with deeper networks (Graph Transformer)
  • Alt-5 (Direct spectral generation): Per-band coefficient generation, bypassing the score network
  • Write up negative result: FANS → graph transfer does not work at small scale with simple score networks
